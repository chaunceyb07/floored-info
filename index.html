The Story

I Am Not
Selling From
The Top.

I do not have the million dollar car. I do not have my own house. I am working a 9 to 5 I do not love. But I wake up at 4:30 every morning with more peace than most people who have everything I do not have yet.

I came home from college after one week. No plan. No momentum. Just a blank page and a choice about what to do with it.

Before that I had been at the top. State champion. Breaking state records in the 4x1. I had won things most people never get close to. And none of it gave me what I was actually looking for.

Because trophies are not peace. Results are not identity. And no amount of winning changes what is happening inside you.

When I came home I asked God for wisdom and discernment. Not money. Not a plan. Just the ability to see clearly. And what happened after that changed everything about how I understand life, business, and what it actually means to build something real.

My daily life feels great right now. Not because I have arrived. Because I stopped chasing the destination and started becoming the person the destination belongs to. I work on my businesses every day. I train every morning. I study the Word. I am building in the process — not waiting for the outcome.

That is what I teach. Not what I achieved. What I am living right now.
